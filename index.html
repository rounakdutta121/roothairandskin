Not every dark spot should
be treated the same way

Freckles, solar lentigines, post-inflammatory pigment, and melasma can look similar but may require different strategies. Treating the wrong pigment can be ineffective or can worsen discoloration. Our team evaluates your skin type, recent tanning, medications, and pigment pattern before recommending XPL.

How XPL targets visible pigment

XPL delivers filtered pulses of light that are absorbed by selected pigment. Treated spots may temporarily darken before gradually flaking or fading as the skin clears targeted pigment. Response varies, and strict sun protection is important.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

XPL is an advanced intense pulsed light technology. Unlike a single-wavelength laser, it uses selected bands of light for appropriate pigment and vascular targets.

No permanent result should be promised. Treated pigment may improve, but new sun damage can develop and some conditions recur. Sun protection and maintenance matter.

Melasma can be complex and may worsen with heat or light in some patients. It must be assessed individually; XPL is not advertised as a universal melasma treatment.

Some targeted spots may darken temporarily before fading. Redness, itching, swelling, or rough texture can also occur.

Candidacy and settings depend on skin tone, pigment type, recent sun exposure, and individual risk. A consultation is essential.
