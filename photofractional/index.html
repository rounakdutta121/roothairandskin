Two Technologies. Different Targets. One Personalized Goal.

XPL and ResurFX™ do not do the same job. Combining them may be useful when both tone and texture concerns are present.

XPL

Visible pigment & redness

Selected for appropriate brown and red targets when tone concerns are part of the clinical picture.

ResurFX™

Texture & collagen renewal

Delivers fractional energy beneath the surface to improve texture and support collagen remodeling.

Process

The PhotoFractional Experience

Comprehensive assessment

We separate pigment, vascular, texture, and wrinkle concerns and screen for contraindications.

Personalized sequence

The team selects XPL and ResurFX™ parameters for your skin and goals.

Treatment

Warm snapping, prickling, or heat may be felt. Comfort and eye-protection measures are used.

Recovery and results

Redness, swelling, itching, temporary darkening, or rough texture can occur. Improvement develops over a treatment series and varies by concern.

Candidacy

Who May Be a Candidate?

A consultation is required. You may be considered if:

  • You have both suitable tone and texture concerns
  • You prefer one coordinated plan rather than unrelated treatments
  • You can allow for the combined recovery response
  • You understand results are progressive and individualized
Clinical note

Treatment may be postponed or changed based on pregnancy status, medications, active infection or inflammation, recent tanning, photosensitivity, prior pigment reactions, or other medical factors. The treating clinician determines candidacy.
