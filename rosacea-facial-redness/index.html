Redness Deserves the Right Diagnosis

Not every flushed or ruddy complexion is the same. Some patterns point to vascular visibility, others to inflammation, barrier stress, or a mix of factors. A careful consultation helps separate what can be addressed with light-based care from what needs medical or skincare support first.

Assessment before any light-based plan
Differentiates vascular vs other redness drivers
Honest guidance on what XPL can and cannot do
Coordinated with skincare and trigger awareness
Clinical clarity An evaluation is the starting point—not a package. Your clinician reviews history, products, and goals, then decides whether Stellar M22 XPL belongs in the plan or whether another approach is safer and more appropriate for your skin right now.

Technology

How XPL Addresses Vascular Redness

Selected light is absorbed by visible vascular targets, generating controlled heat. The body gradually clears treated vessels, which may reduce their appearance. Settings and candidacy must be individualized to reduce the risk of burns or pigment change.

Candidacy

Who May Be a Candidate?

A consultation is required. You may be considered if:

  • You have persistent visible redness or vessels appropriate for light treatment
  • You understand XPL manages appearance rather than curing rosacea
  • You can follow trigger, skincare, and sun-protection guidance
  • You have no contraindication identified during assessment
Clinical note Treatment may be postponed or changed based on pregnancy status, medications, active infection or inflammation, recent tanning, photosensitivity, prior pigment reactions, or other medical factors. The treating clinician determines candidacy.
